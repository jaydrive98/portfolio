About me

Jason Manganyi

Data engineer & BI specialist — I build and run the data platform a whole company relies on, from cloud pipelines to the dashboards leadership opens every morning.

I work end-to-end across data engineering and business intelligence — from production cloud pipelines and platform architecture to executive dashboards, diagnostic analysis and machine-learning forecasting. At VUKA Group I'm the sole architect and owner of a company-wide data ecosystem serving 100+ users and 400k+ CRM records: I set the technical strategy, choose the technology, and enable 30+ stakeholders across six business portfolios.

In practice that means designing the BigQuery data warehouse, building the ETL/ELT pipelines that feed it on Google Cloud, running the HubSpot CRM behind the business, and turning all of it into reporting people actually trust — while keeping everything POPIA/GDPR compliant in a PII-heavy B2B environment. I also manage interns and act as the technical advisor for semi-technical and developer colleagues across the company.

The short version? I like taking messy, scattered data and turning it into something a human can make a decision with. I started out as a commerce generalist — marketing, project management, tutoring — which is exactly why I care so much about the business question behind the query, not just the query.
